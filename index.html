Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is PDF Bundle Builder free?

Yes. The app is free to use. Supporting its development is optional.

Does it work offline?

The PDF tools are designed to process your files on your computer without an internet connection. Downloading and installing the app requires internet access.

Where can I download it?

Choose your platform in Downloads to get the free beta installer.

Will it work on my computer?

The Windows beta is for x64 PCs. The macOS beta is for Macs with Apple silicon.
